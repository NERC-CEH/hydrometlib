# 1.1.0

The energy-balance calculations have moved into `evapotranspiration`, and evapotranspiration from latent heat flux
now returns a documented unit.

## Breaking changes

- **The `flux` module has been removed.** Its two calculations, `latent_heat_flux` and
`evapotranspiration_from_latent_heat_flux`, now live in `evapotranspiration`, alongside the latent heat of
vaporization they depend on. The package now has three modules: `meteorology`, `evapotranspiration` and `cosmos`.
- **`latent_heat_flux` argument renamed.** The soil heat flux argument is now `g` rather than `shf`, matching
`potential_evapotranspiration_30min` and the FAO-56 notation.
- **`evapotranspiration_from_latent_heat_flux` now returns mm h-1.** In 1.0.0 the result had no stated unit and was
computed as `LE / λ / 1000`, which with λ in MJ kg-1 gives g m-2 s-1 (equivalently µm s-1).

## Documentation

- `latent_heat_flux` and `evapotranspiration_from_latent_heat_flux` have fuller reference pages, giving the energy
balance they come from, the assumptions behind it, the unit conversion, and their FAO-56 references.
- Both calculations are listed on the `evapotranspiration` reference page; the separate `flux` page has been removed.
